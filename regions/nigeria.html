Nigeria

Nigeria is Africa’s most populous country. It is home to more than 250 ethnic groups, and English is the official language. The Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is one of the last remnants of primary high forest in southern Nigeria and a living sacred place in Yoruba culture.
