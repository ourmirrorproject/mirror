Region

The Philippines

The Philippines is an archipelago in Southeast Asia; the national mapping agency NAMRIA counts 7,641 islands. Filipino and English are both official languages. For 2,000 years, Ifugao communities have maintained rice terraces that follow the mountain contours of the Cordilleras — a living UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape.
