Region

China

In China, Standard Chinese, or Putonghua, is the official national language; other languages, including Mongolian, Uyghur, and Tibetan, are official in some regions. The country has 61 sites inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List. The Grand Canal, begun in sections from the 5th century BC, later stretched more than 2,000 kilometers, links five major river basins, and is still in use today.
