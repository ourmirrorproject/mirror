Bolivia

Bolivia is a landlocked country in South America. Spanish and 36 Indigenous languages are official under its 2009 Constitution. High on the Andean plateau are Lake Titicaca, shared with Peru and navigable to large vessels, and Salar de Uyuni, the world’s largest salt flat.
