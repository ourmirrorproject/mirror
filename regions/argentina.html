Argentina

Argentina lies in South America, stretching from the Andes to the Atlantic. It includes both the Western Hemisphere’s tallest mountain, Cerro Aconcagua, and the hemisphere’s lowest point, Laguna del Carbón. Along the border with Brazil, Iguazú Falls drop about 80 meters across a span of almost three kilometers.
